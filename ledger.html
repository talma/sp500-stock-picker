Each ranking mode selects its own top N universe and uses that metric for allocation. Each month, a held stock that falls beyond the dip threshold or rises beyond the take-profit threshold is sold; moderate declines receive new capital. Empty eligible slots are filled in ranking order. Missing prices are skipped and their allocation is redistributed among stocks with valid data. Profitable sales incur a 25% tax on realized profit after selling fees, using average cost basis including buy fees; losses receive no tax credit.
